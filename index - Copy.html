Family Heritage

The Story Behind Morbagh

Morbagh was an idea our family had been thinking about for a long time. With our family roots in Rajasthan, we wanted the name and identity of Morbagh to feel authentic and deeply connected to our cultural heritage.

Based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, we decided to bring this idea to life by launching Morbagh online on 11 July 2026.

“Aaj kya pehnu?”

The central thought behind Morbagh is that everyday question women face each morning. We created this brand for women who want clothing that feels authentic, comfortable, and easy to wear in everyday life—whether you are heading to college, work, or spending time with family.
